Felipe Tommaselli

I am a second-year PhD student at the University of São Paulo (USP), advised by Prof. Marcelo Becker and co-advised by Dr. Ricardo V. Godoy. I believe intelligence has natural frequencies in the physical world that we can tune into with the right tools. My research currently focuses on RL for dynamical systems.

I earned a BSc in Electrical Engineering at USP, with a minor in Computer Science, under the supervision of Prof. Robson Cordeiro. Previously, I was a visiting researcher at UIUC with Prof. Girish Chowdhary and worked on AI infrastructure as a software engineer at CloudWalk.

Research

My very recent work includes:

  • whole-body policy learning for locomanipulation with BDs' Spot;
  • contact-rich manipulation benchmarks with the Franka Research 3 [1];
  • understanding on-policy representations and their effect on deployment [2].
